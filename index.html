Cues

Each session uses one cue per topic and rotates through them. Star the ones that work for you and the coach will use them more often.

Songs

Song practice and the song lines after technique work use these. For each song say whether you know it: "Teach me" songs get every line played twice before you sing it, and become "I know it" once you sing the whole song well from memory; "Skip" songs are never used. Pressing next in the first line of a song you know switches it to "Teach me". "Fits" means every note of the song lies inside your measured range; a stretch may reach notes you cannot sing comfortably yet. The built-in melodies are traditional or published before 1929. Only the first words of each phrase are stored, never the lyrics.

Add your own song

At home, never while driving. Type the notes, one phrase per line: note names with the octave (C#4, Bb3), then / and the length in beats if it is not 1, then | and the first few words of the phrase. For example: C4 C4 G4 G4 A4 A4 G4/2 | Twinkle twinkle. It is kept only on this phone and is part of the backup.
