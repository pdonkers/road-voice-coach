## 7 Oct 2026 (version label 2026-10-07.6): more songs, your own songs, songs that fit your range

One backlog item.

Changes:
- Seven songs added to `SONGS`, all traditional or published before 1929, melodies only: When the saints go marching in, Michael row the boat ashore, Silent night, Down in the valley, My Bonnie lies over the ocean, Home on the range, Clementine. Each was transcribed from ABC notation fetched from abcnotation.com and checked against a second transcription, except Down in the valley (one source). Sources, by file: Musica Viva (abc.musicaviva.com, transcriptions by Frank Nordberg) for Saints, Michael row, Down in the valley and Home on the range; Colin Hume's ABC file (colinhume.com/ABC.txt) for Silent night and My Bonnie; Erich Rickheit's abc_dtrad collection (sniff.numachi.com/~rickheit/dtrad) as the second source for Michael row, Silent night, My Bonnie and Clementine; R. L. Walker's ABC files (gulfweb.net/~rlwalker/abc) for Clementine and as the second source for Home on the range; the abcnotation.com "demo/Tunes" files as the second source for Saints and My Bonnie. The URLs are in a comment above `SONGS`. Held notes are cut to 4 beats and rests are dropped.
- Left out: Red River Valley (the sources disagreed on the last line and one had a chromatic note), and Shenandoah (17 semitones), Scarborough Fair (14), Swing Low (14) and Auld Lang Syne (14), which are wider than the app's songs. Three of the new songs span 12 semitones (My Bonnie, Home on the range, Clementine), and the old Are you sleeping spans 14, so they show as "a stretch" for a narrow range.
- Lyrics: `w` is now a cue of 1 to 4 words (for example "Oh when the saints"), never a full line, and the old three songs were shortened too. Song practice says "Now with the words. It starts: <cue>." and the transfer step says "Now the same line with the words. It starts: <cue>." An own phrase with no cue just says "Now with the words."
- Guide, new "Songs" section: one row per song with its span in semitones, "fits your range" or "a stretch for your range" (span at most `high - low - 1` of the measured range; "range not measured yet" without one) and a three-way choice (a select): "I know it", "Teach me", "Skip", stored in `rvc_songKnow` as `{name: mode}`. Unmarked songs are "teach", except Twinkle and Are you sleeping (nursery tunes) and own songs, which are "know"; Amazing Grace is "teach". `songList(p)` gives the songs that are not skipped and fit (falling back to the not-skipped ones, then to all); `songPool(p)` puts the least-sung first (`D.sungS`, counted by name) and, at the same count, known songs before songs to teach. Sorting by "know first" outright would never have reached the songs to teach, so every song comes once, known ones first, before any repeats. `D.song` is now `{s, n, ph}` (name of the song in progress); a state saved before this change maps onto the first three songs.
- Teach me: for a "teach" song `song()` says it is new, then before each phrase plays the line twice on "ah" at the song's tempo ("Listen to this line." then "Once more. Hum along quietly."), and the usual on-la and with-words steps follow. After the whole song is sung from memory with an average error under 50 cents (`analyseSeq` of the take against the song's notes in the key used), the song becomes "know" and the coach says "You know this one now." Next pressed in the first phrase of a song marked "know" (`Skip` caught in `song()`) marks it "teach", restarts it, and says "Okay, I'll teach you that one next time."; pressed in a song to teach, or after the first phrase, nothing changes.
- The transfer step uses only phrases of songs marked "know" (falling back to Twinkle if there are none).
- Guide, "Add your own song" (for home): name, tempo (slow 0.65, medium 0.55, fast 0.45 seconds per beat), up to four phrase lines like `C4 C4 G4 G4 A4 A4 G4/2 | Twinkle twinkle`. `parseOwn()` gives the `SONGS` format relative to the first note of phrase 1 and errors that name the phrase and the token. "Play phrase 1" previews with `play()`. Saved songs are in `rvc_mySongs` (own:true, up to 20), join the rotation and have a Delete button; names must be new. The backup already saves every `rvc_*` key, so own songs and the know / teach / skip choices are in it (checked); "Forget my range, history and takes" leaves them.

## 7 Oct 2026 (version label 2026-10-07.5): ear training by voice, home practice with a live pitch graph

Two backlog items.

Changes:
- Ear training by voice: new `earTrain(p)`, the third exercise of the skills rotation (`skills()` now rotates swell, clean starts, ear training with `D.sk`; the third runs as its own block named "Ear training", with `BLOCK_MIN["Ear training"]` 4.5 and "Skills" lowered to 1.2 from measured times). One block runs three exercises of 4 items each (5 at the top level), answered by singing: "Higher or lower" (two notes, sing the higher; the gap shrinks from 4 to 7 semitones to 1 to 2 over the levels), "Missing note" (three notes of a major scale with a gap, do re _ fa; later other degrees, the gap moved, and downwards) and "Interval by ear" (one note, then "Sing the note a fifth above."; fifths and octaves, then fourths, then thirds). Each exercise has its own level (`D.lv.eh`, `em`, `ei`, 1 to 3, stepping up at 80 percent over 6 or more and down at 50 percent like the others). The sung pitch is judged with `listen` and `analyseHold` against the answer note, within 70 cents with octaves folded, except the octave interval, which is judged without folding (or singing the same note would pass). Feedback is one line ("Right." / "Yes." / "Correct."); after a wrong first answer: "Not quite.", the name of the other note if that was what was sung ("That was the lower one."), the right note played, and one more try ("That's it."). Nothing heard: "I didn't catch that. Once more." Each exercise is explained in full the first time (`D.seen`), afterwards only "Higher one.", "Missing note.", "Interval by ear.". Ear training does not go through `rep()`, so it stays out of the cents-off score and out of the weak-note map `D.notes`; only the first heard answer counts for the level.
- Ear training runs two of its three exercises per block, rotating (`D.earK`): with all three the block measured about 4.9 minutes against the simulated singer, about double the other blocks. Estimate now 3.2 minutes.
- Home practice: new nav button "Home practice" (`start("home")`), run like the mic test: first Stop press ends it at once, and it is not a session, so no singing time (`rvc_sung`), diagnostics, history or practice day is recorded and the daily limit and the cool-down on Stop are not involved. The Home link ends it, as for the mic test; the Mic test button is ignored while it runs. A canvas shows the last 8 seconds of pitch scrolling right to left, a guide line and note name for each semitone of `profile()` (the target's in amber), the line green within 25 cents of the target (octaves folded), orange otherwise, neutral without a target; colours come from the CSS variables each frame, and the canvas follows its width and the device pixel ratio. `tick()` feeds a ring buffer (`HP.buf`) only in this mode, drawn with `requestAnimationFrame`. Controls: target picker with − and + (a semitone, within the range, starting at `profile().home`), "Play note" and "Drone: on/off" (a quiet sawtooth plus sine at the target pitch, following the target). The tuner strip and level bar are hidden in this mode. The nav now has six buttons and wraps onto two lines at 412 px (checked: no sideways scroll).
- Tests: new `tests/ear.js` and `tests/home.js`; `navtest.js` covers home practice; `blocks.js` runs the Ear training block; `timing.js` times swell, clean starts and ear training separately.

Tested in headless Chromium only: `ear.js` (46 checks, 0 failed, 0 page errors: generators at each level and on a narrow range, the 70 cent limit, octave folding and the strict octave, verdicts, the retry, level steps, the full block on the simulated microphone, the rotation, and a 20 minute timed session planning and naming "Ear training"), `home.js` (40 checks, 0 failed, 0 page errors: line pixels read from the canvas, colours, CSS variables, scroll, DPR and resize, picker, Play note, drone, Stop and what it leaves untouched, a session not feeding the graph), `navtest.js`, `length.js 5 8`, `limits.js 10` (59 checks), `focus.js 10` (70 checks) and `technique.js` (79 checks) pass, and `blocks.js` reports 0 page errors. ## 7 Oct 2026 (version label 2026-10-07.4): one focus per session, check the nasality score against your ear

Two backlog items.

Changes:
- Session focus: after the range is known the coach picks one topic from the technique rotation and says it, for example "Today's focus: nasality. You'll do it twice, and it comes first." Both technique blocks of round 1 are that topic (the block rotates its own exercises, so they differ); `D.rotT2` is left alone, so round 2 continues the normal rotation. `pickFocus()` chooses: nasality when the latest nasality score in the history is under 50 or has fallen 8 or more points below the mean of the sessions before it; registers when the notes within 2 semitones of the usual break (`D.brk`) average 25 cents or more off in `D.notes` and are clearly worse than the other notes; otherwise the topic practised longest ago (new `D.lastT={topic:date}`, set when a technique block starts). Nasality is checked first and wins ties; with nasality off, or fewer than two topics, there is no focus. The choice and its reason are written to the diagnostics (`focus: Nasality (nasality score is low, 30)`).
- Timed sessions use the same focus: in round 1 the focus is both technique items of the plan (priority 1 and 4 in `fitRound`), and the line says "twice" or just "It comes first." depending on how many the plan kept, so a five-minute session does not promise two.
- Settings, "Session focus": "Automatic, from my results" (default) or "None, just rotate" (`rvc_focus`); with None the behaviour is as before.
- Nasality score kept with each take: `addClip(label,take,nas)` stores `nas` (0 to 100, or null) on the clip. The lifted ah takes that the nasality block scores are now saved as "Lifted ah" clips with their score; the contrast pair carries no score (the block defines its ends as 0 and 100, so it would only prove itself). Scored takes have their own allowance of the newest 20, apart from the 20 other takes. Backups carry `nas`.
- Progress page: button "Check the nasality measure" opens a new panel (not while a session runs). It plays one scored take (random order, no repeats), asks "How nasal did that sound?" with five buttons from "Very nasal" to "Open, not nasal", keeps the rating in `rvc_nasRate` as `{clip time: rating}` and shows no score. From 8 ratings it shows the Spearman correlation with the verdict (above 0.6 keep it, 0.3 to 0.6 a hint, below 0.3 should be dropped) and a scatter of rating against score. If the verdict is to drop it, nothing is removed; Paul decides. A "Clear my ratings" button starts over.

## 7 Oct 2026 (version label 2026-10-07.3): singing-time limit, two-step Stop, weak-note map

Two backlog items.

Changes:
- Singing time: `listen()` adds the seconds from the first to the last moment the singer was heard (audio clock, so it follows `__speed` like everything else) when it runs in a session; breath takes (the hiss) and mic tests are left out. The day's total is kept in `rvc_sung` as `{d, s, w}` (date, seconds, and `w` once the 80 percent warning was said), reset when the date changes (`today()`, UTC like the rest), and written about every 10 s of new singing and when a session ends.
- Settings, "Daily singing limit": off, 15, 20, 30 or 45 minutes of actual singing, default 30 (`rvc_sungMax`).
- Where every block starts (`block()`, so it covers the timed loop, the untimed loop and the practice blocks alike) the coach checks the limit. At 80 percent, once a day: "About six minutes of singing left for today." (the remaining minutes, rounded, at least one). At the limit: "That's enough singing for today. Your voice has done its work.", then the summary, the cool-down block and the "Finished" end, in timed and untimed sessions. A session started with the limit already reached says "You've reached today's singing limit. Rest your voice and come back tomorrow." right after the calibration and runs no blocks.
- Stop: in a session, once the first block is over (the warm-up, about 2 minutes), the first press of the big Stop button no longer stops. The coach cuts off what it was doing, says "Okay, let's finish with a cool-down.", runs the cool-down with "That's it for today. Well done." as its last line, and the button reads "Stop now". A second press stops at once ("Stopped"); left alone the session ends as "Finished". Before the first block is over, Stop stops at once, as before. A pause is released by the first press. The steering-wheel buttons are unchanged. The first press skips the summary. If the session is already in its own cool-down (timed end, limit), Stop stops at once. `saveSession()` and `diagEnd()` run in every case. Technically: a new `Wind` exception (a kind of `Stopped`) raised by `chk()` when `S.wind` is set, caught in `session()`, which then winds down; `coolDown()` takes the closing line as a parameter.
- Progress page: "Singing today: 12 of 30 minutes." (or just the minutes when the limit is off).
- Weak-note map: `D.notes[midi]={n,err}`, the count and average absolute error in cents for each target note. A plain mean for the first four takes, then `err*0.85+e*0.15`; a note not found in a take that was heard counts as 100 cents. Only the pitch exercises count, the `rep()` calls with a `kind` (notes, scales, intervals, long notes) that are not `quiet` or `noScore`; technique blocks (vowels, onsets, nasality, breath, release, songs, the half-step climb, the transfer step) stay out. `analyseSeq` and `analyseHold` now return the targets and the per-target errors for this.
- Progress page, "Notes": one bar per semitone of the range (`profile()`, low to high), height = average cents off, a note name under every other bar (every third for ranges over 24 semitones), notes with fewer than 3 takes faint, the two weakest notes with 5 takes or more in amber, and a line "Hardest for you: D3 (80¢) and E3 (70¢)."
- Practice aimed at weak notes: in `pitchMatch` and `mixed`, about one target in three (a coin toss at 1 in 3) is one of the 3 notes with the highest error, 5 takes or more and at least 15 cents off, inside that level's range (so level 1 never gets a note far from the comfortable one; with no weak note the target is random as before). The first time in a session the coach says "This one is a note you find harder." Those takes are marked `noLevel`, so the adaptive levels still follow the ordinary notes; they still count in the map and the session average.
- Tests: new `tests/limits.js`.

Tested in headless Chromium only: `limits.js` covers the setting, the Progress line, a session at the limit, the warning and limit line and cool-down in a timed and an untimed session (with the singing total seeded near the limit), the first and second Stop press, a press during a pause, the map arithmetic, real takes from the simulated singer filling `D.notes`, the weak-note generator and the once-per-session line, and the Notes chart (bars, faint and amber bars, names, caption, 412 px width, empty and wide ranges); `blocks.js` and `length.js 5 8` pass. Not tested on the phone or in the car: how the interrupted speech and the "Stop now" button feel at the end of a drive (the cool-down takes about 30 seconds after the first press, so a press at a junction does not end the session at once), whether the singing-time total matches the singer's own sense of time (it is the span from first to last sound in each take, so pauses between notes inside a take count), whether 30 minutes is the right default, whether a weak note comes up often enough to help, and the Notes chart on the phone's own screen. ## 7 Oct 2026 (version label 2026-10-07.2): more nasality exercises, sounds with a job and a half-step climb, transfer to a song

Three backlog items from the YouTube teachers section.

Changes:
- Nasality: besides the contrast pair and the lifted ah, each nasality block now uses one or two exercises from a rotating list of six (`NASX`, counter `D.nx`): sing-ah (as before), "hung" held on the N G and opened to ah, a dark "uh" dropping the jaw into ah, puffed cheeks (feel only: one puffed note, one relaxed, played back, no pitch score), the slow-motion gasp or silent-K in-breath followed by a sung ah, and a dopey Yogi Bear uh-ah followed by a normal ah. The first nasality block of a session uses one, later ones two, so the block stays about as long as before. Sing-ah is now one of the six rather than in every block.
- Five new nasality cues in `TIPS.nasal` (hung then open, dark uh to ah, puffed cheeks, in-breath on a K, dopey Yogi Bear); the slow-motion gasp already existed as "Silent gasp". The Guide lists them with the others.
- The synth has a new vowel shape "uh" and three new demonstrations (hung, uh to ah, Yogi Bear), all built from the existing formant synth.
- New technique topic "Sounds with a job", last in the rotation, so the default rotation has ten slots. Four sounds rotate through `tipFor("job")` (so they can be starred on the Guide page): ng for a relaxed tongue and a forward sound, gee for a loose tongue and bright easy tone, mum with a slight yawn for a low relaxed larynx, a sad no to stop the voice flipping at the break. The coach says the job aloud and plays the pattern on the closest vowel shape (ng, ee, hum, oh), because the synth has no consonants. The pattern is the five-note scale 1-2-3-4-5-4-3-2-1 or 1-3-5-3-1, changing every fourth block.
- The half-step climb is part of that block: the pattern starts at `p.low`+2 and goes up a semitone each step, with "Up a half step." between steps. It stops after two bad misses in a row (not heard, or 60 cents off or more), when the top of the pattern would pass `p.high`-1, or after 8 steps, then says "You climbed to F sharp 3 today." with how many half steps that is above or below last time, and "A new best." when it is. The highest clean step (under 40 cents) is kept in `D.climb`. Climb takes are not counted in the session's cents-off average (new rep option `noScore`), because they deliberately go to the edge of the range.
- Settings, topic checkboxes: the new topic appears automatically. A saved `topics` list now also stores `topicsOf` (the topics that existed when it was saved); a list saved before this change knew the first seven, so the new topic is included until it is unticked. With the default (all topics) it is included.
- Transfer step: about every second technique block (counter `D.xf`; the pattern shifts every 8 blocks so a topic that always lands on the same beat, such as nasality set to "more", still gets it about half the time) ends with one line of a song. The coach says "Now take that into a song. One line of <song>, first on ah, keeping today's cue, <cue name>." (on "mum" for sounds with a job; plain "ah" for nasality, because an M would work against it), the line is sung once on that syllable and once with the words. All 12 song phrases are used in turn (`D.xp`); the key comes from a new shared helper `songTon`, which the song block now uses too. It is inside each technique function, so learned block times include it. In the nasality block it replaces the final phrase-without-M-N-NG step when due.
- Block estimates (`BLOCK_MIN`): technique blocks now include half of the transfer step; new entry "Sounds with a job" 2.3.
- Tests: new `tests/technique.js`; `tests/blocks.js` and `tests/timing.js` include the new block, and `timing.js` also times nasality with and without the transfer step and the transfer step alone.

Tested in headless Chromium only: `technique.js` (79 checks, 0 failed, 0 page errors) covers the exercise rotation, the transfer step text and count, the four sounds in turn, the climb rules with a scripted rep (all clean, two misses in a row, single misses, takes not heard, fair takes, narrow and tiny ranges, nothing clean, higher, same, lower, new best) and the old and new saved topic lists; `blocks.js` passes. `timing.js` at speed 6 with speech stubbed at 70 ms a character: nasality 2.4 min (first block), 2.1 (later), 2.7 with the transfer step; sounds with a job 1.5 (the simulated singer is not on the notes, so the climb stops after two steps; a real climb will be longer, hence the 2.3 estimate); transfer step 0.8. ## 7 Oct 2026 (version label 2026-10-07.1): streak and weekly goal, old take against new take, lesson links

Three backlog items.

Changes:

Mic tests do not count. - Spoken: after the first greeting line, when the streak is 2 days or more counting today, "Day three in a row." (words up to ten, digits after). - Progress page, top: "This week: 2 of 4 days. Streak: 3 days." and the last 8 weeks as small squares (one column of 7 per week, Monday at the top, amber when practised, most recent week last), each week with an accessible label giving its count. ## 5 Oct 2026, commit 4f5e0c8: takes cut off too early

Paul's report after his second drive: listening often stopped far too soon; he could not finish a scale, and was told a sustained note was too short while still holding it.

Causes found and fixed:
1. Clock mismatch in `listen()` (introduced with the background-running change on 4 Oct). Frame timestamps were on the audio-sample clock, but the end-of-take test compared them with the wall clock. Once audio delivery lagged behind real time (any glitch or pause; the lag never recovers), every take ended about 0.6 s after singing began. Reproduced in headless Chromium by injecting 1.5 s of lag (`tests/cut.js`): the old build kept 3 voiced frames of a 6 s note. `listen()` now times everything on the audio clock, with a wall-clock safety limit.
2. Road rumble drowned the voice in pitch detection. The input filter was a single 65 Hz high-pass. In a simulation with noise falling 9 dB per octave and the voice 8 to 11 dB below total noise level, the old filter detected 0% of sung frames; four cascaded 340 Hz high-passes (pitch read from harmonics) plus a 3.2 kHz low-pass detected about 100%. The voicing gate is now 1.5x the noise floor (was 2.5x).
3. Once singing has started, sound above 2.2x the noise floor keeps the take open even when the pitch cannot be read.
4. Held notes are measured from the first to the last frame near the main pitch (`holdSeg`), not the longest unbroken stretch.
5. Longer limits: default end-silence 1.5 s (was 1.2), sequence limit = model length x 1.8 + 6 s, held-note limits raised by about 3 s.

Still unverified on the phone: all of the above in a real car; hum detection may be weaker with the new high-pass because a hum has little energy above 340 Hz.
