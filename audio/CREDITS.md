# Audio credits

The clips in this folder are excerpts from **VocalSet: A Singing Voice Dataset** by Julia Wilkins, Prem Seetharaman, Alison Wahl and Bryan Pardo (ISMIR 2018), version 1.2, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1442513, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

All clips are singer `m1` (baritone). Changes made: trimmed to a few seconds, faded in and out, loudness-normalized, converted to mono 64 kbit/s MP3.
